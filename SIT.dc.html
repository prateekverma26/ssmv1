About

Mandate of SIT Karnataka

Established under the State Support Mission, SIT Karnataka is mandated to act as the State's policy think tank — identifying growth drivers, strengthening monitoring and evaluation systems, and translating data into actionable insight for departments. It convenes government, academia and industry to develop sector strategies aligned with the State's vision for Viksit Karnataka, and channels learnings into the national knowledge platform for other States to adopt.
