NITI–State Workshop Series

The NITI–State Workshop Series, launched under the State Support Mission (SSM), serves as a platform for policy dialogue, knowledge exchange, and collaborative problem-solving between NITI Aayog and States/UTs. Covering themes relevant to socio-economic development and emerging national priorities, the workshops promote cross-learning, generate evidence-based policy recommendations, and strengthen State capacities for effective policy implementation. As of August 2026, over 79 NITI–State Workshops have been conducted across diverse sectors.

State Support Mission

The State Support Mission (SSM) is an umbrella initiative of NITI Aayog that fosters structured and institutionalized engagement with States and Union Territories to support their socio-economic development goals.
